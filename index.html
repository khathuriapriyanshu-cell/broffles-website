Our Identity

The Taste of Good Friendship

At BROFFLES, waffles are more than just a sweet dessert — they are an excuse to bring friends together, share stories, and indulge in warm, chocolatey joy.

Located in the vibrant neighborhood of Kumaraswamy Layout, Bengaluru, we press every waffle fresh to order with precision timing, creating that coveted combination of a crispy outer shell and a tender, steaming center smothered in authentic chocolate fondues.
